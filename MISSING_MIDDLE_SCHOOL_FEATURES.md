# Missing Middle School System Features

This file lists features that are commonly expected in a more complete middle school information system but are not clearly present in the current EduSys feature surface.

## Student Support And Safety

- Behavior, discipline, and incident tracking
- Nurse and health records
- Allergy tracking
- Emergency contact records
- Counseling notes and intervention tracking
- IEP or special education support workflows

## Administration And Operations

- Fees, payments, and billing
- Lunch or cafeteria management
- Transportation and bus route management
- Library, asset, or textbook checkout
- Admissions or registration workflows

## Academic Records

- Report card generation
- Transcript or permanent record exports
- Document export workflows for official academic records

## Communication

- Direct parent-teacher messaging
- Internal staff messaging
- School calendar events beyond class schedules and exam schedules

## Access Control

- More granular staff roles and permissions beyond admin, teacher, student, and parent
- Role-specific permission management for non-teaching staff such as counselors, nurses, finance staff, and transport coordinators
